## Experience

### Bioinformatics Engineer II
**Theiagen Genomics** · 2025 – Present

Develop microbial pathogen genomics pipelines for public health bioinformatics, contributing to team-led pipeline development, cloud job deployment, automated analysis, and AI integration for government and private partners.

### Postdoctoral Researcher
**Dr. Jonathan Jacobs's Emerging Infectious Disease Ecology Lab** · 2023 – 2025

Investigated how transposon-mediated gene loss shapes genome evolution, host switching, and ecological niche switching. Led NGS data standardization and database management, and scaled the Mycotools suite to 100,000s of prokaryote genomes.

### Bioinformatics and Genomics Science Consultant
**Ruby Bio, Inc.** · 2023 – 2024

Integrated metabolomics, transcriptomics, and genomics to identify and engineer expression of biosynthetic pathways.

### Computational Biologist Research Fellow
**Dr. Jason Slot's Fungal Evolutionary Genomics Lab** · 2018 – 2023

Studied the evolution of fungal gene cluster architecture through large-scale genomics and phylogenomics. Developed statistical models of evolutionary processes for biosynthetic gene cluster detection, and built integrative genomics–metabolomics methods that pair phylogenetics with tandem mass spectrometry to discover novel natural products.

### Quality Control Analytical Chemist
**Cascadia Labs** · 2017 – 2018

Performed ORELAP-accredited quantitation and developed LC-MS/MS, HPLC-UV, GC-MS, and GC-FID analytical methods.

### Natural Product Drug Discovery Analytical Chemist
**Dr. Sandra Loesgen's Natural Product Drug Discovery Lab** · 2014 – 2017

Isolated and elucidated the structures of fungal-derived antimicrobial compounds, and developed LC-MS and HPLC-UV analytical methods.
