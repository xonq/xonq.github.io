Artificial Intelligence in Biological Software Engineering

Large language models (LLMs) are indispensable tools that accelerate biological data science and software engineering; however, LLMs are hammers bounded by limited context: they are oracles that will produce the prompted path, and therefore need to be guided by qualified biologists and software engineers to render successful products and research output. I integrate LLMs in my workflow with attention-to-detail paid toward upfront Spec design, establishing essential testing conditions, and conducting rigorous review.
